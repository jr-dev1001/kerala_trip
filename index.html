A wedding, a Fort Kochi sunset and a slow week on the backwaters

There are no flights and no rentals, and every connection has slack. Kochi gets a full afternoon, the Fort Kochi sunset and a night there. You work Monday from Fort Kochi and Tuesday from Alleppey, with a sunset boat ride after work. Then Alleppey is your base for three unhurried leave days: a village canoe, the ₹29 public ferry across Vembanad Lake, and Marari beach. One room for five nights means no repacking, and you're home on Sunday.

Slack built into every day

  • The only two bookings you can't miss are the long sleeper buses, and each has 2–3 hours spare before it.
  • No Kerala leg depends on a single bus. Each one is either a frequent route or has a known next bus.
  • There are at most 2–3 sights a day, with a rest every afternoon and nothing important after dark.
  • Every travel day has a "running late" fallback, so being late costs you one stop, never the trip.

Where you stay

You need 7 paid nights, and Fri 20 and Sat 28 are spent on the bus. Book on free-cancellation rates.

Tirupur · Sat 21

Ask your colleague first, because families often book rooms for out-of-town guests. If not, a budget hotel such as OYO Super Hotel O is 2.9 km from the New Bus Stand.

₹1,467 + ₹155 tax (quoted for 25 Oct), or ₹0 if the family hosts you

Fort Kochi · Sun 22

Stay at Zostel Kochi (Fort Kochi), 8.0/10 from 456 reviews, or The Hosteller Fort Kochi, 8.5 from 191. Both are a short walk from the beach and the fishing nets. You'll work from here on Monday, so ask for a Wi-Fi speed screenshot, and check that reception will hold your bag after check-out.

About ₹900 for a private room (dorm beds are listed from €3.80)

Alleppey · Mon 23 – Fri 27 (5 nights)

One room for five nights, so you never repack. Book a private room for Tuesday's work calls, such as Lake and Paddy Holiday Home or Dazzle Dew, or a dorm at Funky Art Beach House (10/10 from 191 reviews) to save ₹2,250. A middle option is a private room for the two work nights and a dorm bed for the three leave days, at a hostel that has both (₹3,150). Ask for a Wi-Fi speed screenshot and a 5-night rate, and say you'll check in about 9:30 pm on Monday. Anywhere in town works: the boat jetty and the beach are about 3 km apart, and an auto between them is ₹50–80.

₹900 a night private (₹4,500), mixed ₹3,150, or ₹450 dorm (₹2,250) · Hostelworld

Good to know

On the road

  • Alappuzha is flat and compact. Autos start at ₹30 in town; ask them to use the meter.
  • Buses north to Kalavoor, Mararikulam and Ernakulam run along the highway every few minutes. Any bus marked Cherthala or Ernakulam works.
  • Late November can bring showers in Kerala and Tamil Nadu, so keep a rain jacket on top of your bag.
  • Keep ₹500–1,000 in small notes for buses, ferries and autos. UPI works almost everywhere else.

Sights

  • Kottayam ferry (SWTD, ₹29). From Alappuzha: 7:15, 9:35 and 11:30 am, and 2:30 and 5:15 pm. From Kottayam: 6:45 and 11:30 am, and 1, 3:30 and 5:15 pm. These are from a 2024 timetable board, so confirm them at the jetty.
  • Sunset cruise: SWTD "See Kuttanad" boat, daily 5:30–7:30 pm, ₹200. Book on 94000 50326.
  • Alappuzha lighthouse: 9–11:45 am and 2–5:30 pm, closed on Mondays.
  • International Coir Museum, Kalavoor: 9:30 am–5 pm, closed on Mondays.
  • Canoe tours: book through your stay, and ask for the narrow side canals. Mornings are cooler and the light is better.
  • Kerala eateries serve free warm jeera water. It's safer than tap water and saves on bottles.
